Why this matters for pro se filers. A federal complaint is supposed to plead facts, not argue law (Twombly / Iqbal). Even attorneys don't usually load complaints with case citations — that work happens later, in response to a motion to dismiss. Heavy inline citations can actually make a pro se complaint look ghostwritten, which some judges view skeptically and which some districts require to be disclosed. Federal courts do hold pro se filings to a more lenient standard (Haines v. Kerner), so a clean factual complaint is usually the strongest move.
